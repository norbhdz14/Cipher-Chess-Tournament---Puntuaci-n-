Summary:
Chess tournament manager with Swiss system and chess clock

Description:
Cipher Chess Tournament is a native Android application designed for comprehensive chess tournament management using the Swiss system, dynamic pairings, automatic tie-breaks (Buchholz, Sonneborn-Berger), and an integrated digital chess clock with standard time controls (Fischer increment).

Features:
* Automated Swiss system pairings and bye management.
* Instant tie-break calculations.
* Built-in two-sided chess clock synchronized with monotonic time.
* Dark and light themes with English and Spanish language support.
* Data export to TXT and CSV formats.
